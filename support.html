Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Vestara to change plans or cancel.

Canceling keeps your plan active until the end of the current billing period. For refunds, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or switched phones, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you purchased with — active subscriptions are recognized automatically. If something looks wrong, email us.

Notifications

Daily notifications are scheduled on your device. Turn them on or off anytime in iOS Settings → Notifications → Vestara.

Your data

Vestara has no accounts. Your birth details live on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Report a problem

Spotted a bug, a wrong chart detail, or anything that reads off? Email us with your device model and iOS version — screenshots help a lot.
